PRODUCT



Onboarding (how a first-time user feels the value fastest):



1\) Founder onboards quickly with their linkedin profile.



2\) Founder provides the ask (pilot) and upload the details (ask, sector, opportunity, size, etc)



3\) The product matches and show the best fit (Operator) with clear summary of his expertise. AI powered match matching is intent driven based on analysis of the profile as well operator's recent activities.

4\) If the founder like the profile, he simply swipe right.



5\) If the founder doesn't like the profile, he simply swipe left. Next best match is showed.



6\) Founder profile and ask details are shared with the Operator with an Opportunity card showing the founder profile, ask, opportunity details by sourcing and analyzing data from multiple sources.



7\) Once the Operator accepts the request, a meeting is booked based on mutual availability. 

8\) Automatic follow up with till meeting is confirmed.
