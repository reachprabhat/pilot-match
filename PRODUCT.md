## 3\. The core flow

The story: \[The startup founder wants to meet relevant operators/promoters with primary pain points which can be solved by the founder's product as this increase his probability of security a pilot.]

1. The last meeting he had with a senior executive did not result in pilot as the his product main features were not solving the burning pain point of the operator's company
2. He should be able to enter his ask for pilot in plain text for e.g "We have an AI-enabled Supply chain decision intelligence tool that identifies supply chain/production bottlenecks in manufacturing plants. Looking for a 6–8 week pilot with ₹200 Cr+ manufacturers, ideally contract manufactures, retail or industrial products."
3. The AI should read the founder's intent/profile and convert into a structured business opportunity card with brief executive writ up covering problem + proposed solution + pilot + why matched.
4. AI should match with the attributes/profile of onboarded operator/promoters. The fit assessment should be from multiple angles - problem, industry, ICP, company, use case, intent, etc. AI should dynamically modify the business opportunity (based om features offered only) card to make it appealing for the operator.
5. To the founder, AI should present the best match operator/promoter with Pilot fit score along wit the reason for the same.
6. The founder should be able to choose "Request to meet" or "Park" or "Reject"
7. Once the founder has selected "Request to meet", AI should automatically send a notification along with the structured business opportunity card to the selected Operator. Structured business card should not reveal the name of founder/startup yet. Also, the business card should be customized based on operator's profile and intent.
8. The operator should be able to view the business opportunity card along with option to "Interested" or "Not Relevant".
9. If the operator selects "Interested", send one AI-generated introduction to both along with name and contact details on their respective WhatsApp contact number.

Below steps are nice to have and are to be parked for future development

13. If the response is "yes", check what was the outcome. Provide option to choose from - Exploring Pilot, Follow up call planned, Good meeting but no pilot, Misfit.

Dev-only extension: operators submit name, company, role, city, WhatsApp, LinkedIn profile URL, self-reported annual INR revenue band, up to three pilot preferences, up to three help areas and required contact-sharing consent. Applications are Pending until the owner approves from the existing private admin link. Only approval inserts a new operator into the existing matching table. Pilot preferences map to industry, help capabilities to about; companyProblems stays not found. No automatic enrichment, no signup AI calls and no login. Existing operators and all personal links are preserved. Participant contacts remain restricted to each Accepted pair.


Signup refinement: new applicants choose exactly one Your industry entry, saved on approval in the existing industry field. Optional pain points up to 200 characters are saved in the application and mapped to companyProblems on approval; blank means not found. Help areas continue mapping to about, not to companyProblems. Existing matching reads both fields without logic changes. Pending-to-approved owner flow unchanged; old applications remain approvable without editing their records. Dev only.
